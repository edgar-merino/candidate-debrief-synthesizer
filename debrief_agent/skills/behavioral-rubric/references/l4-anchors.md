# L4 Behavioral Anchors (Cloud AI Engineer / TSC)

## Core Competencies

### 1. Ownership & Execution in Ambiguity
- **Positive Signals:**
  - Describes taking end-to-end responsibility for a well-scoped feature, customer issue, or technical deliverable, including navigating unexpected blockers.
  - Clearly articulates personal actions ("I") and measurable outcomes rather than speaking only in generalities.
- **Negative Signals:**
  - Waits for explicit step-by-step instructions when requirements shift or blockers arise.
  - Attributes failures solely to external teams or teammates without acknowledging personal accountability.

### 2. Cross-Functional Collaboration & Conflict Resolution
- **Positive Signals:**
  - Listens to differing viewpoints, uses data or technical prototypes to align peers, and resolves disagreements constructively.
  - Proactively communicates status, risks, and trade-offs to teammates and immediate stakeholders.
- **Negative Signals:**
  - Escalates minor disagreements immediately or avoids raising legitimate technical risks to keep the peace.
  - Dismisses partner/customer perspectives without investigating underlying needs.

### 3. Customer Empathy & Technical Communication
- **Positive Signals:**
  - Adapts technical explanations to the audience (engineers vs. non-technical stakeholders) and translates customer pain points into actionable engineering work.
  - Follows through on commitments and validates that the delivered solution actually solved the user's problem.
- **Negative Signals:**
  - Uses jargon without checking comprehension or focuses solely on technical elegance over customer impact.
  - Struggles to explain why a past project mattered to users or the business.

### 4. Growth Mindset & Reflective Learning
- **Positive Signals:**
  - Openly shares a real mistake or failed assumption, explains what they changed afterward, and shows concrete evidence of applying the lesson.
  - Seeks and acts on constructive feedback from peers or mentors.
- **Negative Signals:**
  - Offers a disguised strength ("my weakness is working too hard") instead of a genuine learning moment.
  - Repeats past patterns without demonstrating self-awareness or behavioral change.
