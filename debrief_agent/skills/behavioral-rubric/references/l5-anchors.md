# L5 Behavioral Anchors (Cloud AI Engineer / TSC)

## Core Competencies

### 1. Navigating High Ambiguity & Strategic Ownership
- **Positive Signals:**
  - Demonstrates ownership across multi-team or high-stakes customer initiatives where goals, architecture, or success criteria were initially undefined.
  - Proactively identifies systemic risks, establishes clear milestones/metrics, and drives alignment through measurable business or engineering outcomes.
- **Negative Signals:**
  - Operates only within pre-defined tickets or cannot articulate their individual leadership impact in a complex multi-team effort.
  - Fails to quantify outcomes or connect technical work to broader organizational goals even after probing.

### 2. Influence Without Authority & Conflict Resolution
- **Positive Signals:**
  - Resolves high-stakes technical or roadmap disagreements across teams/customers using evidence, trade-off analysis, and shared goals rather than hierarchy.
  - Builds durable consensus while making difficult trade-offs explicit (e.g., pushing back on scope or timelines with credible alternatives).
- **Negative Signals:**
  - Relies on manager escalation to resolve cross-team friction or forces a technical preference without stakeholder buy-in.
  - Compromises critical quality/security standards just to avoid difficult conversations.

### 3. Executive & Customer Communication
- **Positive Signals:**
  - Tailors complex AI/cloud architecture trade-offs, risks, and ROI crisply to executive, product, and engineering audiences alike.
  - Manages difficult customer expectations transparently during incidents, delays, or scope pivots while preserving trust.
- **Negative Signals:**
  - Overwhelms business stakeholders with low-level implementation details or obscures project risks until late in delivery.
  - Struggles to articulate clear recommendations when facing competing stakeholder priorities.

### 4. Elevating Others & Continuous Learning
- **Positive Signals:**
  - Actively raises team capability through mentoring, design reviews, reusable frameworks, or blameless postmortems that prevent recurring failures.
  - Demonstrates high self-awareness around major judgment calls, articulating concrete systemic changes made after setbacks.
- **Negative Signals:**
  - Focuses exclusively on individual heroics with no evidence of multiplying teammates' effectiveness.
  - Rationalizes setbacks externally or offers surface-level lessons without lasting process or behavioral change.
