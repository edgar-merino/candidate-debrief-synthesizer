# L3 Behavioral Anchors (Cloud AI Engineer / TSC)

## Core Competencies

### 1. Task Ownership & Reliable Execution
- **Positive Signals:**
  - Takes clear ownership of assigned tasks, internships, or academic/team projects and follows through to completion.
  - Clearly distinguishes personal contributions ("I") from group work and proactively raises blockers early.
- **Negative Signals:**
  - Leaves tasks unfinished without communicating status or waits passively when blocked.
  - Speaks only in vague "we" terms and cannot explain what they personally built or fixed.

### 2. Teamwork & Constructive Collaboration
- **Positive Signals:**
  - Collaborates respectfully with peers, asks thoughtful questions, and responds positively to code reviews or differing ideas.
  - Shares credit generously and helps unblock teammates when able.
- **Negative Signals:**
  - Reacts defensively to feedback or avoids communicating when a disagreement arises.
  - Works in isolation and surprises teammates late in a project.

### 3. Clear Communication & User Focus
- **Positive Signals:**
  - Explains technical work clearly and concisely without unnecessary jargon.
  - Shows curiosity about end-user needs and validates that their work solves the intended problem.
- **Negative Signals:**
  - Struggles to structure a coherent story (missing context, actions, or outcome) even after interviewer probing.
  - Focuses solely on tools/tech without understanding why the work mattered.

### 4. Coachability & Learning Agility
- **Positive Signals:**
  - Shares a genuine mistake or knowledge gap, explains how they sought help or researched a fix, and describes what they do differently now.
  - Demonstrates eagerness to learn new technologies and apply mentor feedback quickly.
- **Negative Signals:**
  - Avoids admitting mistakes or blames teammates/tools without personal reflection.
  - Shows little evidence of learning from past feedback or adapting their approach.
